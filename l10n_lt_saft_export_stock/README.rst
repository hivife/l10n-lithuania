**i.SAF (monthly invoice register) is in development and will follow in
a separate module.**

Adds stock data to the Lithuanian SAF-T file of ``l10n_lt_saft_export``:
movement types, physical stock (opening / closing quantity and value)
and movement of goods. Installed automatically together with
``stock_account``.
